Monster Energy Landing Page

A modern and visually striking Monster Energy landing page UI design created in Figma. The design focuses on bold typography, product presentation, strong colors, and a clean modern layout.

🎨 Design Variations

The landing page includes three visual variations:

🟢 Black / Green – Classic Monster-inspired design with a bold green background.
⚪ White – Clean and minimal design with a light background.
🌸 Pink – Vibrant pink design with a colorful product presentation.

This project is a Monster Energy landing page concept designed for a modern beverage brand website.
