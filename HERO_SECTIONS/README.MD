Hero Sections Design Collection

A collection of modern hero section designs created in Figma, showcasing different visual styles, layouts, and product-focused landing page concepts.

Projects Included
Hoodies — Fashion-focused hero section featuring a bold headline, product imagery, and promotional call-to-action.
Perfume 78 — Minimal fragrance landing page with product photography, elegant typography, and a clean aesthetic.
Strength & Grace — Editorial-style hero section featuring a strong visual composition and a premium, inspirational theme.
Shoes — Product-focused footwear hero section designed for a modern fashion/sportswear presentation.
